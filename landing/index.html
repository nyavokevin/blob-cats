Field notes

Round on purpose
No corners to bump into, no edges to get wrong. Blob is a soft egg with two ears and several opinions.
Mostly air
Breathes slowly, bounces when it laughs, squashes when it lands. That's the whole skeleton.
Looks up, never at you
Unless you move. Then every eye in the herd follows your cursor, reluctantly.
